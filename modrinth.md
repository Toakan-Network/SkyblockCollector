# SkyblockCollector

Track the Hypixel SkyBlock collections the Collections menu doesn't cover.

- **Trophy Frogs and Fish:** set a goal tier and see only what you're missing, with how to catch it.
- **Accessories:** see your power total and what to get next: what you can make from items you hold, what you can
  afford now, then the rest, ranked by coins per power.
- **HUD panel:** keep your missing trophies and next accessories on screen. Move and resize it, or place it with Huddable.

Press **J** or use `/skyblockcollector` on SkyBlock. Read-only and client-side.

Requires Fabric API, Hypixel Mod API and SkyblockDB.
